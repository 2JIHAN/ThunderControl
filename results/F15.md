# F15 timestamp semantics
Goal: check F15 locally before .38 without assuming capture-time PTS is better.
VERIFIED offline semantics; production PTS UNCHANGED. Live SCK-clock correspondence and pre-callback age remain INCONCLUSIVE.
No violated contract or benefit requiring real capture PTS was established. No FPS or latency claim.
Source: FrameSink advances sequence only for complete frames with pixels; VT receives ordinal/targetFPS, first PTS zero.
SubmitAt.captureSequence uses the PTS numerator. It is a frame ordinal, not host ticks; keeping PTS unchanged preserves this meaning.
Now() uses systemUptime; capturedAt starts when OfferCapture runs, after callback guards. It does not measure pre-callback age.
Raw SCK PTS and DisplayTime are logged, not interpreted as the Now() clock. Invalid/indefinite/infinite raw times cannot become VT PTS here.
Idle callbacks do not advance sequence. Busy replacement can skip complete-frame ordinals. Codec replacement preserves submittedSequence.
Encoded constructs the wire payload without VT sample PTS; DisplayVideoDecoder recreates local sequence/30 sample timing and immediate presentation.
Applied only Tests/F15NativeVTTimestamps.m. No production file, queue, guard, slot, wire, codec selection, quality or synthetic mode changed.
Fixture wraps only VT session creation to observe real samples instead of packet/writer callbacks; calls production InitEncoder and SubmitAt.
